## Result definitions

- **Meaningful reply:** contains a workflow, problem, correction, or buying/testing signal; not a like or generic compliment.

- **Application:** a Founding Trucks inquiry/application attributable to the post or UTM.
- **Partner call:** a scheduled conversation with a relevant organization or commissary.
