# ORGANIZATION-PARTNERSHIP-PLAN — Free member pilot

**Version:** 1.0
**Updated:** 2026-09-21
**Owner:** Founder

## Goal

Reach US food-truck operators through organizations they already trust. The first
ask is an expert review, not an endorsement. If the tool is useful, run a small
member pilot before asking the organization to distribute it more broadly.

## The free offer

Two parts must be described separately:

1. **Public tools:** Temp Danger Zone Checker and Inspection Readiness Quiz are
   free to use without creating an account.
2. **Founding Trucks cohort:** eligible partner members may apply to the same
   20-operator cohort used across every acquisition source. Accepted operators
   receive the full Solo Truck product free for 3 months, with no card required,
   in exchange for short weekly feedback.

Do not call the full product “free.” The standard public offer remains a 14-day
no-card trial; current paid pricing after trial is $24/month or $190/year.

There are not separate 20-person pools for social and each organization. Five of
the 20 total places are temporarily reserved for organization-referred applicants;
the other 15 are available to social/direct applicants. Capacity and release rules
live in `FOUNDING-TRUCKS-TRACKER.md`.

## Who to contact first

### Tier 1 — national organizations

1. **NSFVA:** first outreach because its published mission emphasizes education,
   community, and vendor support. Address the message to Executive Director Bill
   Moore if that contact is still listed when sending; otherwise use the current
   general contact.
2. **NFTA:** second outreach. Ask for a review of the two free tools and whether a
   small member pilot would be useful. Do not claim its audience size or say it is
   seeking vendors unless the organization confirms that directly.

Always re-check the contact page on the day of sending. As of 2026-09-21, the
official pages list `BillMoore@nsfva.org` / `info@nsfva.org` for NSFVA and
`info@nationalfoodtrucks.org` for NFTA.

### Tier 2 — regional organizations and commissaries

After one of the two national emails is sent, build a list of five regional food
truck associations or commissary/shared-kitchen networks in the first target
state. Use `outreach/organization-partnership-template.md`; do not copy one generic
email to all five.

## Three-stage partnership path

| Stage | Ask | What Solo Truck gives | Gate to advance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Review | 10–15 minutes of expert feedback on one free tool | Free tool access; no member data requested | Reviewer says the tool is relevant and flags no material issue |
| 2. Pilot | Refer interested members to the shared cohort, subject to remaining capacity | 3 months free, no card; onboarding and support | At least 3 operators activate and provide feedback |
| 3. Distribution | Share an approved resource or case study | Member resource, results summary, optional co-education | Written permission for names, logo, quotes, and distribution language |

No affiliate fee, exclusivity, member-data access, logo use, or public endorsement
request in the first email.

## 30-day execution

| Window | Action | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–2 | Test live URLs and personalize NSFVA email | One reviewed email sent |
| Days 3–5 | Personalize and send NFTA email | Second reviewed email sent |
| Days 6–10 | Research five regional organizations/commissaries | Five qualified records with current contact sources |
| 5 business days after each email | Send short follow-up if there is no reply | Reply or follow-up logged |
| 7 business days later | Send final close-the-loop note | Close, refer, or schedule a call |
| Days 15–30 | Run review calls or prepare a small pilot | Clear yes/no/next step for every active lead |
| Day 30 | Close silent leads and reconcile partner holds | Only explicit, unexpired holds remain; unused capacity is released |

## Call agenda (15 minutes)

1. Two minutes: who Solo Truck is for and what it does today.
2. Five minutes: reviewer uses one free tool or sees the daily-log flow.
3. Five minutes: ask what is inaccurate, missing, or unsuitable for members.
4. Three minutes: agree on one next step—stop, revise, or invite a small pilot.

Questions to ask:

- Which member workflow is this most relevant to, if any?
- What language would create compliance or liability concerns?
- Would a small referred-member pilot be useful, and how many candidates could you
  realistically reach?
- What evidence would you need before sharing it with members?

## Measurement and decision rules

Track the funnel as: **delivered → replied → reviewed → pilot agreed → operator
activated → permission to distribute**.

| Metric | 30-day experiment threshold |
|---|---:|
| Qualified organizations contacted | 7 |
| Human replies | 2 |
| Review calls | 1 |
| Pilot conversations | 1 |
| Activated partner-sourced operators | 3 |

These are decision thresholds, not forecasts. Continue the channel if an
organization agrees to review or pilot. If all seven contacts are silent after two
personalized follow-ups, pause national outreach and test the same offer with
regional associations or commissaries before changing the product.

At Day 30, any lead that received the final note and still has not replied becomes
`Closed — No response` and holds no cohort places. A positive partner conversation
may hold only an explicitly agreed number of places and must have a `Hold until`
date in `FOUNDING-TRUCKS-TRACKER.md`. Expired holds return to social/direct unless a
new date is explicitly agreed. Every release transfers the same capacity from the
organization allocation to social/direct; total cohort capacity remains 20.

## Guardrails

- Verify every URL, product feature, price, offer, contact, and organization detail
  on the day of sending.
- Never imply endorsement, partnership, FDA approval, or guaranteed inspection
  outcomes.
- Do not share member/operator data with an organization without explicit consent.
- Use only real usage numbers, quotes, case studies, and shipped features.
- Treat local health requirements as local; direct operators to their health
  authority for authoritative guidance.
- Get written permission before using an organization’s name, logo, quote, or
  member list in marketing.
- Check `FOUNDING-TRUCKS-TRACKER.md` before promising or accepting a pilot place.
