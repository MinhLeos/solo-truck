# Day 23 — What happens without a signal?

**Platform:** Facebook Group
**Objective:** Validate offline workflow needs
**Link:** None

## Post

How reliable is mobile data where you prep and serve?

I’m asking because a record-keeping tool that works only with a perfect connection may be useless at exactly the wrong moment. Solo Truck is designed to queue entries offline and sync them later, but I want to understand the real failure modes.

Do you lose signal at the commissary, inside the truck, at events—or rarely enough that offline support does not matter?

## Publish and reply

- Say “designed to queue and sync later,” not “never loses data.”
- Ask about device and location only if relevant; do not collect precise location data.
- Track how often connectivity actually disrupts work.
