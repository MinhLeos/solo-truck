# Day 01 — Where do your records live?

**Platform:** Facebook Group
**Objective:** Learn the current workflow
**Link:** None

## Post

Food truck owners: where do your daily temperature records actually live by the end of a busy week?

Paper clipboard, notebook, spreadsheet, phone notes, another app—or a mix of all five?

No judgment and no pitch. I’m building for small mobile-food operations and want to understand the real workflow before assuming the problem is “just use an app.” What works well in your current system, and what tends to fall apart?

## Publish and reply

- Adapt the first line to the group; do not cross-post identical wording.
- If asked, disclose: “I’m the founder of Solo Truck and I’m doing workflow research.”
- Ask one follow-up: “What happens when you need to find an older record?”
- Track: group, date, meaningful replies, exact phrases, DMs.
