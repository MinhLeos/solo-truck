# Day 20 — Handing over records

**Platform:** Facebook Group
**Objective:** Understand retrieval and sharing needs
**Link:** None

## Post

When someone authorized asks to see recent operating records, what is the smoothest handoff you can realistically make today?

Hand over a binder? Scroll through a phone? Email a PDF? Pull files from several places?

I’m interested in the mechanics—not asking anyone to share private inspection details. What format is easiest for you to prepare, and what part takes the longest?

## Publish and reply
