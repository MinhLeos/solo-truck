# Day 09 — Small operator, small tool

**Platform:** LinkedIn founder profile
**Objective:** Clarify product scope
**Link:** None

## Post

Software for a restaurant group and software for one food truck should not begin with the same assumptions.

A chain may need sensors, roles, locations, training modules, and enterprise reporting. A small operator may need one fast action between prep and service: record what happened, then find it later.

Solo Truck is deliberately scoped to the second job. That means saying no to features that make the interface impressive but the daily action slower.

For people who support small operators: where have you seen “enterprise best practice” create unnecessary work for a one-to-three-person business?

## Publish and reply

- Avoid naming or attacking competitors.
- Do not present “small” as unsophisticated; the distinction is workflow complexity.
- Track examples that reveal scope boundaries.
