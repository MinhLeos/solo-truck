# Day 24 — What Inspector Mode is meant to do

**Platform:** LinkedIn founder profile
**Objective:** Explain a shipped workflow without overclaiming
**Link:** Optional real demo

## Post

One Solo Truck workflow is called Inspector Mode.

The job is simple: bring temperature logs, corrective actions, and checklists from the last 30 or 90 days into a read-only view, with a PDF export when a separate file is useful.

It organizes records already entered. It does not certify them, guarantee acceptance, or replace the operator’s local requirements.

That distinction matters. Good compliance software should make evidence easier to retrieve without pretending the software is the authority.

For people who review operational records, what makes a digital handoff clear and usable?
