# Day 04 — Your pre-opening routine

**Platform:** Facebook Group
**Objective:** Learn how operators prepare for service
**Link:** None

## Post

Before service starts, do you work from a written opening checklist or mostly from memory?

For people using a list: is it paper, a phone note, a form, or part of another system? For people using memory: which check is easiest to remember too late, once the shift is already moving?

I’m trying to understand what a checklist has to look like to help without becoming one more piece of admin.

## Publish and reply

- Do not prescribe a universal checklist; local and operational needs differ.
- Ask: “Who completes it when the owner is not opening?”
- Track whether the need is reminders, ownership, speed, or proof.
