> Full disclosure up front: I'm a solo developer, not a food truck owner. I'm building a small tool for daily compliance logging (temps, checklists) because I kept seeing the same gap while talking to truck owners this week.
>
> Existing options are either a one-time HACCP binder setup (good for day one, doesn't help you log daily) or enterprise platforms built for restaurant chains at $169+/month with sensors and staff training nobody running one truck needs.
>
> I'm not here to tell anyone how to run their truck — I'm here to listen to what makes the daily compliance side harder than it should be, and build carefully around that.
>
> If you run a truck or cart solo, what's one thing existing compliance apps get wrong about your day? Genuinely asking, no pitch.
