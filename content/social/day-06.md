# Day 06 — What I need to validate

**Platform:** LinkedIn founder profile
**Objective:** Make the research questions public
**Link:** None

## Post

This month I’m not trying to validate “people dislike paperwork.” That is too vague to build from.

I’m trying to answer four more useful questions:

1. Where do independent operators record daily checks now?
2. What prevents a record from being completed during a busy shift?
3. What do they need to retrieve quickly later?
4. Will a lightweight tool earn a place in that routine?

Solo Truck succeeds only if it reduces friction in a real shift—not because the feature list sounds sensible on a landing page.

What question is missing from this validation plan?

## Publish and reply

- Do not attach a signup CTA.
- Prioritize comments from people with direct operator or support experience.
- Record challenges to the thesis, not just agreement.
