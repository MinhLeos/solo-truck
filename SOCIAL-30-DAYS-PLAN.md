# SOCIAL-30-DAYS-PLAN — Active plan

**Updated:** 2026-09-21

This document is the short index for the active 30-day campaign. The earlier
Instagram/TikTok/Reddit plan has been retired because it conflicted with the
founder’s available time and the current platform decision.

## Active channels

- **Facebook Groups:** 20 operator-facing posts.
- **Founder LinkedIn profile:** 10 evidence/partner-facing posts.

Organization outreach is a separate plan, not a social platform. See
`ORGANIZATION-PARTNERSHIP-PLAN.md`.

## Source of truth

- Strategy, audience, claims, measurement: `SOCIAL-MEDIA-STRATEGY.md`
- Schedule and results tracker: `SOCIAL-CONTENT-CALENDAR.md`
- Ready-to-post daily copy: `content/social/day-01.md` through `day-30.md`
- Shared Founding Trucks capacity: `FOUNDING-TRUCKS-TRACKER.md`

## Execution rules

1. Fill the community register and warm up groups before Day 1.
2. Personalize every Facebook post for one group; do not cross-post identical copy.
3. Replace `[REAL ...]`, `[FOUNDER NAME]`, and URL placeholders before posting.
4. If real evidence is unavailable on a recap day, skip or rewrite the post—never
   publish a placeholder or invent a result.
5. Check group rules and the live product before every product/tool post.
6. Review results weekly and update upcoming hooks without changing historical
   published copy.

## Campaign outcome

At Day 30, make a keep/reduce/stop decision for each platform using downstream
actions—operator conversations, tool use, applications, activations, or partner
calls—not reach or likes alone.
