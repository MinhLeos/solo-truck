# Reusable organization outreach template

Use this for a regional association, commissary network, shared kitchen, training
provider, or other operator-serving organization. Complete the research card before
writing the email.

## Subject

Could you review a free resource for [MEMBER TYPE]?

## Initial email

Hi [FIRST NAME],

I’m [FOUNDER NAME], founder of Solo Truck, a lightweight daily record-keeping tool
for independent food trucks and mobile food units.

I found [ORGANIZATION] through [SPECIFIC PROGRAM/RESOURCE]. Your work on [TRUE,
SPECIFIC TOPIC] is why I’m reaching out.

Before asking you to share anything, would you be willing to review this free,
no-signup [TOOL NAME]?

[VERIFIED TOOL URL]

I’m looking for a candid answer: is it useful and appropriately framed for your
members? It is a reference tool, not a substitute for local health authority
guidance.

If it passes your review, eligible members may apply for reserved places in our
single 20-operator Founding Trucks cohort, subject to availability and review.
Accepted operators receive the full Solo Truck product free for 3 months, no card
required, in exchange for short weekly feedback. No endorsement or exclusivity is
expected.

Would a 15-minute review next week work, or is there a better person to ask?

Best,
[FOUNDER NAME] · [EMAIL] · [VERIFIED PRODUCT URL]

## Follow-up — 5 business days later

Hi [FIRST NAME],

Just bringing this back to the top of your inbox. I’m asking for a resource review,
not an endorsement. If [TOOL NAME] is not relevant to your members, a quick “not a
fit” is genuinely useful. If someone else owns [education/member resources], would
you point me to them?

Best,
[FOUNDER NAME]

## Final note — 7 business days later

Hi [FIRST NAME],

I’ll close the loop after this. If now is not the right time, no response is needed.
Thank you for the work you do for [MEMBER TYPE].

Best,
[FOUNDER NAME]

## Rules

- Keep the personalized initial email between 120 and 170 words.
- One recipient, one relevant reference, one primary CTA.
- Never invent familiarity, member counts, needs, or interest.
- Never ask to use a name/logo or request member data in the first email.
- Verify the recipient, link, offer, and shipped feature on send day.
- Check `../FOUNDING-TRUCKS-TRACKER.md` before describing place availability.
