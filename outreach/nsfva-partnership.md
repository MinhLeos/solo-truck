## Email Body

Hi [Name],

I'm building **Solo Truck** — a free, offline-first daily compliance logging tool for food truck and mobile food unit operators in the US.

**Why I'm reaching out to NSFVA specifically:**
Your organization has been leading the charge on compliance education, regulatory transparency, and member support. You've invested heavily in producing guides, educational videos, and webinars on inspection readiness and health code compliance.

I built Solo Truck to solve a problem your members face constantly: **turning daily compliance data into inspection readiness.** Every feature is directly informed by operator feedback on what actually works in the field.

**What Solo Truck offers:**
- **Daily compliance logging:** Temperature + corrective actions + time-stamped documentation
- **Offline-first architecture:** Food trucks have weak signals; logs sync when connectivity returns (this matters)
- **Free public tools** (no signup):
  - Temp Danger Zone Checker (FDA Food Code reference tool)
  - Inspection Readiness Quiz (12-question assessment → downloadable PDF)
- **Founding Trucks program:** Free 14-day trial for operators willing to provide feedback

**Why this complements NSFVA's mission:**
NSFVA teaches compliance principles and inspection readiness. Solo Truck is the operational tool that makes that education actionable — it's not a replacement for your guidance, it's what makes following your guidance actually *easy* for operators who are time-constrained and running on limited tech infrastructure.

**What we're proposing:**
1. **Educational integration:** Feature the free Temp Danger Zone Checker + Inspection Readiness Quiz in NSFVA learning resources (with attribution)
2. **Co-authored content:** Partner on a compliance checklist or inspection prep guide (NSFVA expertise + Solo Truck implementation)
3. **Member benefits:** NSFVA members get priority access to Founding Trucks program or featured positioning
4. **Community feedback loop:** NSFVA members help shape roadmap (you already have the strongest operator network)
5. **No exclusivity:** We're vendor-agnostic — if another tool serves members better, that's fine

**Why now:**
We're launching a 30-day founder outreach campaign specifically with food truck operators and mobile food vendors, grounded in the communities you already lead. NSFVA partnership would mean this reaches the right people with the right message.

**Next step:**
I'd love a 20-minute call to walk through the free tools and discuss how we might collaborate. Whether it's content integration, member benefits, or just ensuring Solo Truck stays aligned with industry best practices.

**See it yourself:**
- Free tools: www.solotruck.app/tools
- Try it: www.solotruck.app

Looking forward to exploring this together.

## Why NSFVA is Higher Priority than NFTA (Arguably)

1. **Compliance = Core Mission:** NSFVA's entire identity is compliance education. Solo Truck is a natural extension of that mission, not a side tool.
2. **Content Production:** They're already producing compliance resources — co-authoring or integrating with them is lower friction.
3. **Smaller, More Agile:** Easier to get buy-in vs. NFTA's larger bureaucracy.
4. **Educational Alignment:** Teaching operators how to be inspection-ready is exactly what NSFVA does — Solo Truck just makes it operational.

**Recommendation:** Send NSFVA first, or in parallel. If NSFVA bites, it becomes a case study for NFTA outreach ("Already partnered with NSFVA...").
